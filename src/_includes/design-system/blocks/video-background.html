{%- comment -%} Video background block - a full-width video playing silently behind overlaid text content. The video starts on its own and takes no interaction. A thumbnail covers the iframe until the video is playing. For a YouTube video, design-system.js listens for the player to start and fades it out. Parameters (via block object): - block.video_id: YouTube video ID, or the embed URL of another provider (required) - block.video_title: Accessible title for the iframe - block.thumbnail_url: Image path or URL shown while the video loads. Defaults to YouTube's own thumbnail for a YouTube ID. - block.class: Extra classes for the container - block.reveal: Sets a data-reveal animation on the container - block.badge / block.content / block.buttons: Hero-style content laid over the video, rendered by design-system/media-background-overlay.html {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _thumbnail = block.video_id | videoThumbnail: block.thumbnail_url -%} {%- assign _is_youtube = block.video_id | isYouTubeId -%}
