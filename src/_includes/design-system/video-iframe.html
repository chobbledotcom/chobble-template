{%- comment -%} Video iframe with privacy-protecting defaults, shared by the video blocks. Parameters: - video_id: YouTube video ID, or the embed URL of another provider (required) - name: Accessible title for the iframe (required) - mode: "click" (default) for a video the visitor started, "autoplay" for one that starts muted when it scrolls into view, or "background" for one that loops silently behind content. A background video is not lazy-loaded: it must load to start playing. {%- endcomment -%}
